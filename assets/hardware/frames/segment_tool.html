Left-click an object/part to propose a segment (green dot = include). Right-click (or shift-click) adds a red exclude dot to refine the proposal. The blue overlay is the candidate. Press 1 / 2 / 3 to switch between the three SAM granularities (small part → whole object); the default is the highest-confidence one. Enter adds the candidate to what stays visible, Backspace removes it from what is visible. Everything not kept turns white in the preview. S saves the masked image (and the mask) to disk.
